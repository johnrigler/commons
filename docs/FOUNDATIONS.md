# Foundations, articles and cases

Recorded October 21, 2026. John Rigler's Commons architecture direction.

**Foundations are broad subjects or organizing concerns. Articles are the
reusable documents that develop a particular issue.** An article can connect
multiple foundations. A case can reference multiple articles. Commons is a
network of these relationships rather than a directory tree in which every
article has exactly one parent.

## The example that defines the model

Real Estate and Religion can be separate foundations. A proposed **Parsonage
Exemption** article connects both. Its placement does not require deriving it
from the administrative-burden document. This is an organizational example;
no substantive exemption rule or tax guidance has been drafted or verified.

| Article | Foundations or concerns it can connect | Status |

| Unsupported rent acceleration | Real Estate and procedural burden; credit-reporting consequences where relevant. | Reusable reading draft exists; individual evidence remains separate. |

| Veterans dealing with the VA | Veterans' affairs and procedural burden. | Proposed case/article area; identify the particular decision and procedure. |

These connections describe relevance, not a finding about an individual case.
An article does not have to link to every foundation. A foundation may have its
own introduction without being identical to one fixed document.

## The existing burden document

**Deterrence through administrative burden** is the formal title of the document
John has referred to as the sludge document. Sludge is the shorter term for
excessive or unjustified procedural friction. It can support articles or cases
about credit reporting, traffic tickets or VA processes without becoming the
single root of all Commons material.

The traffic-ticket inquiry examines which burdens discourage a challenge and
how record or insurance consequences depend on resources. Arizona diversion in
the [reading companion](../reading/assistance/) documents one mechanism; it does
not identify John's case or establish uniform purposes or rules for traffic
systems. Insurers' downstream use of risk information differs from credit
bureaus' record-maintenance and distribution role.

## Articles and individual cases

An article explains a reusable issue and identifies its scope and sources. An
individual case adds the actual chronology, evidence, questions and requested
action. The case may cite several exact article editions where relevant.

The religious-exemption subject is another possible article area within Religion,
with scope and sources still to be selected. It need not connect to the burden
article. Parsonage Exemption illustrates an intersection, not a new legal claim.

## Publication structure

Keep articles identifiable by title, author, scope, sources and exact edition.
Retain the established numbered source parts and immutable snapshots. Existing
HTML articles remain the concrete documents. This note records the relationship
model; it does not claim a topic-filtering interface or new catalog schema has
already been implemented.
