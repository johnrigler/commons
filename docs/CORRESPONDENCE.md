# Common correspondence and private completion

A Commons correspondence entry should preserve a reusable explanation, question
or request. It can be assembled into a precise message for a particular person
or company without publishing the individual case.

## Default construction

1. Identify the message's recipient, purpose, disputed proposition and requested
   answer in the private case.
2. Select reusable Commons explanations and references. Preserve nuance; generic
   wording need not be vague or reduced to a slogan.
3. Leave sender, organization, employee, account, amounts, document references,
   event dates and case facts blank in the public template.
4. Print the blank letter and fill it by hand, or fill the same fields privately
   in the browser. The printed form should already provide useful writing space.
5. Sign, retain the completed original, make a copy and send the copy. Keep the
   exact completed correspondence, attachments, delivery and response together
   in the private case. Actual dispatch is a separate recorded event.

Private browser filling changes the working copy only. It should not save values
into a public contribution, URL, remote service or the blank source. A public
signed template, where supplied, remains blank; consumer signatures belong to
completed derivatives. No author signature, pin or dispatch is inferred.

## Inquiry about a signed document

Say directly that the person digitally signed the identified document when that is what
the source establishes. Ask in what capacity they signed and whether the content
and action reflect company policy, delegated authority or personal initiative.
Keep the original document and exact signature evidence in the private record.

The purpose is to determine responsibility fairly. Avoid assuming a signer
created a policy or committed a wrong, and avoid changing their act of signing
into mere approval of some separate document. Ask the organization to identify
its policy, instructions, responsible function and route to a substantive answer.

Company profiles and public research can name organizations when their purpose
requires it. They remain separate records from the reusable correspondence;
their names do not become prefilled values in a generic letter.

## Professional handoff

A professional should be able to find the chronology, exact source records,
reusable argument and questions still unanswered. This can reduce repeated
explanation and reconstruction. The professional still determines the claims,
forum, remedies and procedural requirements. Explain technical preservation or
verification when it helps the recipient assess the record; the broader Web3 and legal-cost rationale may be stated openly in the project
README and contributor notes. It is not automatically boilerplate in every letter.

The first generic form is [Clarify the document, policy and authority](../entry/authority-clarification.html).
Its standalone browser page is [the correspondence form](../reading/correspondence/index.html).
It supports a two-page handwriting copy, private electronic fields and email-text
preparation. It does not send mail, file a claim, determine liability, or calculate
a statutory deadline.
