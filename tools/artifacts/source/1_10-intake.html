Keep the artifacts separate

Document 1 is the exact signed source. Document 2 is the related correspondence. Each has its own SHA-256. A downloaded pair record connects them; its hash identifies that record, rather than either source file.

The default transaction payload identifies Document 1. To anchor the correspondence separately, select Document 2 and prepare another transaction. To commit to both in one hash, select the pair record and retain the downloaded record with the originals.

A hash commitment provides a reference for checking exact bytes later. It does not itself establish who sent or received a letter, a document's original date, or the truth of its contents.
