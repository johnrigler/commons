# PTSD, functional limitations, and disability benefits

An entry under **Disability & public benefits**, connected to the existing
evidence, assistance, review, state-program, and access-burden articles.

The article covers civilian and military PTSD, diagnosis and benefit standards,
the two routes in SSA listing 12.15, functional documentation, treatment gaps,
agency evidence development, and the separate VA compensation system. It does
not make a personal diagnosis, eligibility finding, or claim that a difficult
procedure necessarily reflects deliberate exclusion.

- Portal entry: `ptsd-disability`
- Standalone reading edition: `index.html`
- Research: NIMH, SSA, and VA primary sources checked 22 October 2026
- Authoring: numbered HTML parts with PHP assembly and ordinary CSS/JavaScript
- Publication record: `edition.json` and its frozen CID copy
- Ledger preparation: `examples/ptsd-disability.draft.json`, unsigned

The reader and standalone edition support browser printing. Personal clinical
and trauma records remain outside this public explanation. Historical frozen
editions are preserved; pinning, funding, signing, and broadcast have not been
performed or represented as completed.
