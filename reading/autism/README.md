# Autism, assessment, and access to disability benefits

The first entry in the Commons **Autism** subject connects childhood screening,
clinical assessment, adults without an earlier diagnosis, and benefit evidence.
It is also indexed under **Disability & public benefits** and connected to the
existing evidence, assistance, state-program, and access-burden articles.

The article explains that autistic people can qualify for disability benefits
when their documented limitations and other circumstances meet the program's
rules. It separates screening, diagnosis, service eligibility, and benefit
decisions; distinguishes adult and childhood standards; and describes the
work-related evaluation after an adult impairment does not meet a listing.
The discussion does not assume a personal diagnosis or publish private records.
