To Experian · Regarding National Credit Systems

Unsupported Rent Acceleration

Request for reinvestigation and correction or deletion of an unsupported reported balance.

I ask Experian to reinvestigate the accuracy and completeness of the balance reported by National Credit Systems and attributed to Firmus, obtain the records supporting the disputed charges, and correct or delete any reported amount that cannot be substantiated. Please address the specific issues raised in my dispute and provide the results in writing.

A central issue is unsupported rent acceleration: rent charged in advance for a remaining lease period without sufficient supporting records to evaluate the amount claimed. My request concerns the basis for those charges, the calculation used, and the credits or adjustments that should affect the reported balance.

The broader concern is deterrence through administrative burden: a process whose unnecessary complexity, repetition, delay, or expense discourages a person from pursuing a substantive answer. Unsupported rent acceleration can operate through this mechanism when the difficulty of challenging a demand gives it leverage before its basis has been demonstrated.

The individual case document presents the chronology, correspondence, disputed entries, and supporting evidence. The shared explanation in the Commons provides background for the issue. The evidence in the individual case establishes how that issue applies here.

I am using written correspondence to preserve a complete, readable account of the dispute and the material supplied with it. Identification material belongs in a separate private packet for matching the submission to my credit file.
