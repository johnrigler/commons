# Disability benefits and the work of access

Seven connected articles examine how eligibility rules reach people through
applications, medical evidence, agency assistance, deadlines, and review. New
Mexico's General Assistance provides a concrete state example. A separate
comparison explains the shared federal SSDI/SSI framework and different New
Mexico and Texas Medicaid routes.

The live portal indexes each article under **Disability & public benefits**,
opens it in the reader, and supplies its sources and connections. The standalone
`index.html` edition also supports browser printing. Numbered source parts and
ordinary CSS/JavaScript assemble with the repository's SB Shell publisher.

The argument distinguishes eligibility from application completion and funding
limits. It treats evidence-development duties as shared where the rules provide
for agency help. Published office-closure research supports the access-cost
mechanism; it does not establish current New Mexico/Texas performance or a
deliberate exclusion strategy. Policy comparisons are dated 22 October 2026.

`edition.json` and its frozen CID copy identify the articles, sources, and
publication status. `examples/disability-access.draft.json` prepares an unsigned
ledger reference. No pinning, funding, signing, or broadcast is represented as
having occurred. Personal health and claim evidence belongs in a private case
record, rather than this public research collection.
