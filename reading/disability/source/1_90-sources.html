Disability access: research and program rules

Federal standards, state programs, empirical findings, and editorial analysis are identified separately.

  1. Manasi Deshpande and Yue Li, “Who Is Screened Out? Application Costs and the Targeting of Disability Programs,” American Economic Journal: Economic Policy 11(4), 2019, pp. 213–248. Published estimates concerning SSA field-office closures. The 10% and 16% findings are study estimates, not current state-specific rates.
  2. SSA, Disability Evaluation Under Social Security, Part I: General Information. Federal adult definition, different childhood standard, and program responsibilities.
  3. SSA Red Book, Overview of Disability Programs. SSDI insurance conditions, SSI financial conditions, and concurrent eligibility.
  4. SSA, Disability Determination Process. Field-office and state DDS responsibilities in federal claims.
  5. 8.106.500 NMAC, General Assistance benefit and eligibility provisions. Section 8: state funding limits; current compilation includes amendments effective 1 March 2025. A rule permitting suspension is not evidence of a current suspension.
  6. New Mexico Division of Vocational Rehabilitation, Disability Determination Services. State DDS role in Social Security claims.
  7. 8.106.420 NMAC, Requirements for Determining Disability. Sections 8–9: case development, medical statements, current evidence, applicant responsibilities, and department assistance. Agency payment for an examination is funding-dependent.
  8. SSA, Disability Evaluation Under Social Security, Part II: Evidentiary Requirements. Impairment, severity, medical and nonmedical evidence, and assistance obtaining reports.
  9. SSA, Medical Evidence. Do not delay filing for records; sources, agency requests, examinations, and information about function.
  10. 20 CFR §404.1512, Responsibility for evidence. Title II claimant duties, complete submissions, medical-history development, and reasonable efforts to obtain reports.
  11. 8.106.110 NMAC, General Assistance application procedures. Sections 9–13: right to apply, form assistance, interview hardship alternatives, medical reconsideration, delay, and written notice. The 15-day request and 30-day evidence periods run from the denial date.
  12. SSA, Request reconsideration. Disability and nonmedical reconsideration requests; 60 days from receipt of the decision.
  13. 8.100.970 NMAC, Administrative Hearings. Section 9(A)–(B): request methods and 90-day adverse-action notice period; separate program and service rules can apply.
  14. SSA, Electronic Appeal filing guidance. Alternative filing when required information cannot be supplied within the appeal period.
  15. SSA POMS GN 03101.020, Good Cause for Extending the Time Limit to File an Appeal. Relevant barriers, written explanation, assistance, and an individualized agency determination.
  16. SSA Publication 05-10075, Your Right to Representation, December 2024. Ordinary assistance, formal appointment, representative functions, and fee authorization.
  17. New Mexico Aging and Long-Term Services Department, Options Counseling / ADRC. Long-term service and support navigation, Medicaid and Medicare counseling. This service description does not promise representation in a federal disability appeal.
  18. Disability Rights New Mexico, Intake and Referral Process. Free confidential services, priorities and capacity limits, no prior SSA finding required, and no representation created merely by contacting the organization.
  19. Texas HHSC, MEPD Handbook A-2100, Supplemental Security Income. Revision effective 1 September 2025: automatic Medicaid eligibility for SSI recipients and the state data interface.
  20. Texas HHSC, MEPD Handbook D-2300, Requesting a Decision from the Disability Determination Unit. State Medicaid medical determinations, records, and forms H3034/H3035. This DDU route is distinct from the federal claim described by SSA.
  21. 8.296.400 NMAC, Medicaid Eligibility—Other Adults. Section 9 lists this category's conditions without a disability-proof requirement; other ACA requirements still apply.
  22. KFF State Health Facts, Status of State Action on the Medicaid Expansion Decision, October 2026. KFF's original state-policy tracking: New Mexico adopted expansion; Texas had not. A dated policy comparison, not an approval-rate study.
  23. Texas HHSC, MEPD Handbook M-3200, Disability. Medicaid Buy-In's disability standard and substantial-gainful-activity exception. Other program conditions remain separate.
  24. Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs, Tools for Serving People with Disabilities. Identifies ADRCs as information and benefit-assistance resources.
  25. SSA POMS SI DAL01730.008, New Mexico SSA/State Agreement under Section 1634. Automatic Medicaid eligibility for SSI cash and Section 1619(b) recipients, subject to stated limitations. Reviewed policy dated 30 May 2024.

The proposed access audit, the organization of an applicant's records, and the coverage/evidence feedback argument are editorial analysis. The office-closure study supports a mechanism, not a current ranking of states. This edition records selected rules as checked on 22 October 2026; individual notices, benefit categories, and decision stages determine which procedures apply. No independent legal review is recorded.
