This form keeps the request clear: investigate the specific error, consider the supporting records, and correct or delete inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable reporting as appropriate. It has room for one consumer or two people disputing the same item in their separate files. The second signature is optional.

Write the actual facts in your own words. The charge rows can describe rent, fees, payments, credits or another disputed item. The optional fraud-referral paragraph applies only when its statement is true. Attach copies of relevant records and separate identification/address information for each signer, using Experian's current mail instructions. Keep the originals and a copy of what you send.

The public record is the blank template. Names, account details, evidence and consumer signatures belong to your completed copy. Filling this page changes only the preview in this tab; it does not publish or submit anything. Print or save your completed copy before closing the tab.

Request for a substantive written result

Review the written dispute

  1. Investigate the specific facts. Review the records relevant to the reported item and the error described on page 1. For rental charges, review the applicable lease terms, dates, calculations, payments, deposits and credits, including mitigation and reletting adjustments where applicable. A list of posted charges alone does not resolve a dispute about their basis or the missing adjustments.
  2. Preserve the substance of this dispute. Consider the enclosed information and send all relevant dispute information to the reporting company under 15 U.S.C. §1681i(a)(2) and (4). Any selected reason or summary should accurately convey the actual objection. Please identify the reason or classification transmitted.
  3. Correct or delete as appropriate. Correct inaccurate or incomplete reporting. Delete or modify information that cannot be verified under §1681i(a)(5)(A). If no accurate balance can be verified, I/we request deletion of the collection item.
  4. Give each signer the written result. Send the results and revised consumer report for each signer's file under §1681i(a)(6). If the item remains, explain how the specific disputed facts were resolved and identify the records relied on; provide copies if available. For any completed reinvestigation identified below, please also provide the procedure description, including the contacted furnisher's business name, address and telephone number if reasonably available, under §1681i(a)(6)(B)(iii) and (7).

Earlier requests / completed result, if any

I/we prefer written correspondence. Referral to the reporting company or its collection portal does not answer the specific issues stated here. If information is missing, please identify it so it can be supplied in writing. If you maintain that a call or portal submission is required, please explain the basis in writing.
