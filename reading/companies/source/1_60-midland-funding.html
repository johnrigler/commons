The author's view

Opinion · 22 October 2026

I object to treating a company's name on a credit report as the answer to a disputed ownership question. Midland Funding's relationship with a servicer explains a business arrangement. It does not, by itself, trace the particular account through a sale or establish the amount claimed.

My standard is an account-specific explanation that survives outside the buyer's or servicer's system. If the consumer must enter another institution-controlled workflow just to reconstruct that explanation, the cost of making the claim understandable has shifted onto the person being pursued. The shared portal argument and traceable-account article describe the problem I want this profile to expose.

Identity and business role

Midland Funding LLC is described by its affiliate's official material as a purchaser and owner of accounts charged off by original lenders. Midland Credit Management, Inc. (MCM) services Midland Funding accounts and accounts MCM itself owns. The site identifies both within the Encore Capital Group family.[13] These relationships explain why a communication may name more than one business.

Selected official record: a 2020 judgment

The CFPB's 2020 action named Encore Capital Group, Midland Funding, Midland Credit Management, and Asset Acceptance Capital Corp. It alleged violations of an earlier order and federal consumer law. On 16 October 2020, the court entered a stipulated final judgment requiring $79,308.81 in consumer redress and a $15 million civil penalty, with disclosure and other conduct provisions.[15] Separately, the administrative docket records termination of the 2015 consent order on 20 October 2020.[16] These are distinct procedural events; the 2015 order should not be described as still in force solely because it appears in historical background.

Connect the buyer to the account

The traceable-account article asks how a claimed transfer identifies the specific account. Naming a debt buyer supplies an entity reference. The sale records, account identifiers, balance history, and other case evidence must still establish the particular claim.

MCM's Midland Funding page provides its public explanation and contact route.[13] A company statement that a reported name indicates ownership is not a substitute for examining the actual disputed record.
