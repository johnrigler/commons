John Rigler’s view

Opinion · 22 October 2026

I do not accept a dispute system's own description as the measure of whether a consumer can effectively challenge it. My test is whether the person can state the actual objection, preserve the submission, identify the source, and obtain a response that addresses the contested fact.

TransUnion's consumer guide expressly describes choosing a prepared statement or writing one's own.[17] That matters: the interfaces should be compared accurately. The wider criticism still applies. Free text alone does not establish that an attachment reached the investigator, that the submitted text remains available to the consumer, or that the underlying objection was answered. Those are the questions I want asked of all three agencies in When the portal controls the objection.

Identity and business role

The 2023 record discussed here names Trans Union LLC and its then-subsidiary, TransUnion Rental Screening Solutions, Inc. The CFPB describes the latter as providing tenant and employment background reports to property owners, managers, employers, and other screening businesses.[6] The profile's entity reference uses Trans Union LLC as named in that record. A tenant-screening report and a nationwide credit report should each be matched to the issuer shown on the document.

Selected official record: a 2023 settlement

The CFPB and FTC filed a joint complaint on 12 October 2023 alleging inaccurate eviction reporting and inadequate disclosure of third-party record sources. The court entered a stipulated order on 18 October 2023 requiring accuracy and disclosure measures, $11 million in consumer redress, and a $4 million penalty.[6] The order states that the defendants neither admit nor deny the allegations, apart from specified jurisdictional facts.[7] Its scope concerns the named defendants and background-screening practices.

The consumer-facing process

TransUnion's consumer support page separates disputes, credit freezes, fraud alerts, and subscription support.[8] The CFPB also lists credit-dispute routes.[5]

Follow the information to its source

The connection to traceable records is practical: an adverse report becomes harder to challenge when the source, event, or outcome cannot be reconstructed. A case should preserve the actual product, issuer, record source, disputed field, and decision. The company profile makes that organizational background reusable across different disputes.
