# Company profiles

Seven profiles form a distinct catalog class and a separate **Companies** view:
Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, National Credit Systems, Rausch Sturm LLP,
Midland Funding LLC, and Midland Credit Management, Inc.

Each identifies the legal entity in scope, its business role, selected dated
primary records, official routes, and related Commons arguments. Company
statements, allegations, procedural records, and settled findings retain their
different meanings. The current verified Rausch Sturm name is not treated as
proof of a party's identity in a remembered suit.

- Directory: `../../index.html#view=companies`
- Standalone reading edition: `index.html`
- Sources: 16 primary references checked 22 October 2026
- Sources and assembly: numbered HTML parts, PHP, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- Edition: `edition.json` and its immutable CID copy
- Prepared ledger reference: `../../examples/company-profiles.draft.json`, unsigned

Profiles export `commons/company-reference/1` JSON so a later case can reference
the organization separately from a general argument. The case still needs its
own evidence of the organization's involvement. A reply to a profile remains
an ordinary contribution, not a company profile.

Historical snapshots are retained. The reading edition supports browser
printing. No live IPFS pin or blockchain broadcast is represented as completed.
