07 · After a case

Public Records, Settlements and Reusable Precedent

A case does not have to be financially large to produce an inspectable record. But different outcomes have very different legal effects.

OutcomeWhat it normally establishesUse by others
Private settlementParties resolved the matter under negotiated terms; may disclaim liability.Often limited information, possibly confidential.
Public dismissal after settlementDispute existed and was disposed of.Other lawyers can cite the docket as history, not assume wrongdoing was found.
Justice court judgmentDisposition binding on parties subject to applicable review/appeal.Public record usually retrievable; no general binding precedent.
Trial court findings/judgmentSpecific findings if actually entered, not merely allegations in filings.Potential evidence/research lead in another matter; rarely binding on strangers.
Published appellate opinionJudicial legal reasoning that can carry precedential weight within a jurisdiction.Can be cited as authority, with weight dependent on court and jurisdiction.

Texas justice-court judgments may be appealed to county court for trial de novo under applicable procedures. A county/district judgment is not automatically a published appellate precedent. A case's legal importance depends on what a court actually decided and what source material is public, not merely the dollars at issue.

Public evidence is not everything discovered

Discovery may reveal collection policies, contract terms, training materials and communications. Protective orders and sealing rules can restrict disclosure; a litigation exhibit attached to a filing may not be evidence admitted by the court; and a court might enter a short judgment without detailed explanation. The published dossier should clearly distinguish pleading, declaration, exhibit, admitted evidence, order, and judicial findings.

What the institutions might do

A judgment could motivate NCS or a creditor to review policies or retrain staff, but does not automatically force system-wide reform. A properly pleaded and proved claim could seek prospective relief where authorized. A settlement could provide fast individual relief while limiting public findings. Neither guarantees that another consumer will have a claim without proving its own facts.

Commons as the durable public edition

  1. Keep an index of public court records and exact source citations.
  2. Preserve private source artifacts and redactions separately; do not expose account numbers or verification answers.
  3. For every public artifact, store its source role, date, exact digest and revisions without overwriting its antecedent record.
  4. When appropriate, publish an exact signed/hashed edition and record any IPFS CID or Litecoin transaction as a separate event; never imply one happened until verified.
  5. Record later appeals, settlements, corrections and rebuttals as dated additions, not silent revisions to the claims about the earlier record.
