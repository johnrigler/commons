NCS, Firmus, and the Test of Debt Verification

Can a collector discover that its creditor is wrong? This dossier separates a disputed apartment-rent calculation, the collector's review process, credit-reporting consequences, prior litigation, and what it would take to establish a creditor's knowledge.

Public working dossier, not a legal finding. The account concerns a disputed $10,365.56 rental collection described by the author. Some chronology derives from documents and an unverified machine transcript; other facts remain the author's account. No court has adjudicated this particular dispute on the material reviewed here. Editorial method

Distinguish source record, reported recollection, published adjudication, plausible legal theory, and unanswered question. In particular, an adverse decision against NCS in another case is not a finding in this dispute, and a creditor's receipt of litigation records is not automatically actual knowledge of repeated violations of Texas Chapter 392.
