Specific unresolved conflicts

An initial representative appeared to describe a dispute as already resolved; a later manager identified the October 26 submission as newly pending. These could be different disputes, rather than proof of false statements. The call also shows that an unrepresented consumer did reach a collection manager; it does not establish a categorical bar on managerial access. Whether that manager had independent decision authority remains unanswered.

Requested underlying records

  • Lease and addenda, original ledger, charge method, vacancy records, replacement-tenant dates, replacement rent and credits.
  • NCS dispute-intake records, full submissions and attachments, review logs, decisions, written responses and furnishing history.
  • Original call audio and metadata, human-checked transcript, correction log, and a separate redacted public derivative if authorized.
  • Three-bureau credit reports, deletion notices, date-stamped score history and adverse-action evidence if any.
