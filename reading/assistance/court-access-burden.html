The cost of procedural access

A filing route can exist on paper and remain costly to use. Record the difference between what a procedure permits and what it takes to complete it.

Author identity: LcudkPQzLuuqsnzHSmJ7iLREaHStvPKRVb · AI-assisted draft · Unsigned; not an on-chain or CID-registered edition

A filing-fee waiver, an online court portal, and the possibility of a remote hearing address different costs. None tells us by itself whether a person can identify the correct court, prepare the proper document, transmit it successfully, serve the other party, or obtain a meaningful decision. A person in a rural area may have limited access to printing, scanning, travel, broadband, or paid help.

The Commons administrative-burden argument asks how many steps, hours, fees, repeated submissions and unanswered questions stand between an objection and review. This worksheet makes that inquiry observable rather than assuming an inaccessible process or a legal violation.

Two measurements, one event record

DimensionWhat to recordAvoid confusing it with
Formal accessPublished route, form, rule, eligibility condition, filing method, waiver, hearing option; source and date checkedProof that this person successfully used that route
Practical accessActual attempt, required equipment, time, travel, out-of-pocket expense, failure, repeat work, staff instruction and resultA universal requirement or systematic denial based on one incident
Institutional decisionWho accepted, rejected, redirected or resolved the submission, on what stated grounds, with what written recordAn inferred motive or a legal finding
Effect of assistanceWhether a fee waiver, e-filing, local assistance or remote appearance was requested, available, granted, and actually usedAssuming a stated option eliminated every related cost

Audit rule

Separate the source of a rule from the outcome of a particular attempt. Keep the original court notice, submission receipt, returned filing, email, travel receipt and contemporaneous observation as distinct evidence. A step marked unknown is not a failed step; a missing receipt is not proof the recipient received nothing. A denied fee waiver does not by itself establish an invalid court process, and a waived fee is not zero procedural cost.

Record the institutional explanation for each obstacle, any lower-cost route genuinely available, and whether that alternative worked. An avoidable barrier is a hypothesis to test against the actual rule and purpose, not a label automatically attached to an inconvenient requirement.

This is a blank research and preparation form, not a court petition, fee-waiver application, legal advice or evidence of an actual filing. Complete identifying facts privately. A Commons publication is not a court submission.
