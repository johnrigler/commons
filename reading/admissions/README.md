# Admissions, ownership, and debt claims

Two connected Commons articles preserve Rigler's account of repeatedly disputing
proof of transfer while acknowledging past creditor relationships. They examine
how a hardship explanation can contain a factual concession without treating
every statement as conclusive proof of every part of a debt claim.

The feed entries are `debt-admissions` and `debt-ownership`, under **Courts & civil
process** and **Housing & credit**. The companion distinguishes party statements,
judicial admissions, formal discovery admissions, assignment challenges, and
justice-court ownership requirements. Current Texas sources provide context;
the precise case, governing historical rules, statements, and basis of decision
are unverified. The FTC's 2010 report documents related information concerns;
it does not establish that this specific pattern is the norm.
