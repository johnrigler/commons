# Collector: written inaccuracy dispute and supporting-record request

**Use and scope.** Complete the actual disputed item and the reason it is inaccurate. This version is directed to a third-party collector for which Texas Finance Code section 392.202 applies. Coverage depends on the actual facts. The express citation clarifies the requested procedure; it does not make a prior uncited notice invalid. Supporting-record requests are separate from the statute's written-statement deadline. See the [working document](pre-suit-dispute-working-document.md).

The objection concerns the legal and factual basis for the amount after mitigation, reletting, payments, deposits and credits. Repeating the posted balance does not explain those disputed facts.

Please record and investigate this written notice of inaccuracy under Texas Finance Code section 392.202. Please send the statement required by subsection (b) no later than the thirtieth day after receipt, admitting or denying the inaccuracy or stating insufficient time to complete the investigation. Please comply with the applicable investigation, collection-cessation, correction and notification requirements of that section. If you state insufficient time, please comply with subsection (d) rather than treating the dispute as unanswered indefinitely.

Please also provide the following supporting records and explanations so the disputed balance can be reconciled:

1. The executed lease and all relevant addenda, including concession provisions.
2. The complete resident ledger from move-in through collection placement and later adjustments.
3. An itemized breakdown of charges, payments, deposits, refunds, credits and reversals.
4. The acceleration, reletting, early-termination and concession provisions relied on, with the dates and calculation applied.
5. Records showing when the unit became available for re-rental and the reletting efforts made.
6. Records showing whether and when it was re-rented, the replacement lease start date, relevant replacement rent and the resulting credits.
7. Support for disputed cleaning, paint, damage, reletting or other non-rent charges.
8. Your authority to collect, the current creditor, and the party authorized to correct, withdraw, recall or settle the account.
9. An explanation of what information was considered in investigating the specific objection stated above, including any information obtained from the source party.

Please distinguish existing historical records from a later reconstruction. If requested records do not exist or cannot be located, please identify which records and explain that fact. Redact unrelated tenants' personal information while retaining the relevant dates and financial terms.

If this account is furnished to a consumer reporting agency, please record and communicate that the amount is disputed and make any correction required by the applicable law. If you maintain that the amount is accurate, please explain how the disputed mitigation and credit issues were addressed.

Please send a written response identifying whose dispute and account file were reviewed. Where both consumers join, I/we request a response addressing each consumer's stated dispute at the listed correspondence address.

## Retain privately

Keep the exact signed letter, actual enclosures, mailing record, delivery evidence and later responses. Certified mail with return receipt can help establish receipt; it does not expand the statute into a demand for every document within thirty days. Do not enter a dispatch, receipt or deadline that has not occurred or been established.
