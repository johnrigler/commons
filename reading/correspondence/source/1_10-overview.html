The questions preserve the distinction between the person's act of digitally signing, their capacity as an employee, company policy, delegated authority, outside instructions and individual initiative. The inquiry asks for an explanation in good faith while pressing for a substantive organizational answer. It does not assume personal wrongdoing from a signature.

Use this wording when the person digitally signed the identified document. Describe the specific rental-charge objection and attach copies of the relevant signed source and earlier requests. The signer is asked about that document itself, rather than hypothetical approval of some other document.
