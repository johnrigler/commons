# Owner or manager: rental balance and post-mitigation accounting request

**Use and scope.** This is directed to the property-side source of the rental balance. It preserves the accounting, mitigation and reletting questions. Texas Finance Code section 392.202 is a third-party-collector procedure and does not automatically impose its statement deadline on every owner or manager. Other duties depend on the recipient's actual role and conduct. See the [working document](pre-suit-dispute-working-document.md).

Please identify the period charged as future rent and show the calculation of the amount after mitigation and credits. In particular, please provide:

1. Records of actual mitigation and reletting efforts for the unit.
2. The date the unit was ready and made available for re-rental, with any relevant availability restrictions.
3. Whether and when the unit was re-rented, the replacement lease start date, the relevant rent and concessions, and the credits attributable to the disputed period.

5. Support for any disputed cleaning, paint, damage, reletting or other charge.

## Retain privately

Keep the exact signed letter, actual enclosures, mailing and delivery records and every later response. The landlord's mitigation duty under Texas Property Code section 91.006 is distinct from a fixed deadline to disclose every requested record. This letter does not assert that silence alone proves no mitigation, cancels the balance or establishes damages.
