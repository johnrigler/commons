Two documents. Exact bytes.

Inspect an original digitally signed PDF and related correspondence locally, then pass a readable SHA-256 commitment to Chisel.

Open the two-document artifact intake →

Choose the original signature-bearing PDF as Document 1 and a saved letter or email as Document 2. Each keeps its own full SHA-256. The tool reads metadata and embedded signature information without rewriting the sources. The files and extracted details stay in the browser tab.

The default anchor identifies the exact bytes of Document 1. A separate choice prepares Document 2 or the downloaded pair record that connects both. The payload is readable ASCII: sha256: followed by 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters, 71 bytes in total. CertLedger is the transaction's transport tag; Chisel handles output amounts, funding, fees, review, signing and broadcast.

Only the selected hash crosses the Chisel handoff. Save the private pair record with the source files in the intended case record. Extracted names, dates, PDF properties and email headers are source metadata. This intake does not verify digital signatures, certificate trust, revocation or mail delivery, and prepares no claim that an unbroadcast draft is already on-chain.
